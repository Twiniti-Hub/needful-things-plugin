Your Needful Things account, inside Grok Build.

Browse categories and listings by region, check your posting credits and posts, and prepare drafts and publication proposals that you approve in the Needful Things app.

What it does

Browse by region.

Search and read categories and listings in your account's region: EU, UK or US.

See your account at a glance.

Read your posting credits, your own posts, favourites, saved searches, drafts and chats.

Drafts and proposals, your approval.

Create and edit drafts, then propose publication or an extension. Nothing publishes and no credit is spent until you approve it in the Needful Things app.

Set up in three steps available from launch, 28 Oct

  1. Install the plugin and pick your region. In Grok Build, open the Marketplace tab (type /marketplace), select needful-things and press i. Or, from the terminal, run grok plugin install needful-things --trust. Then set NT_MCP_URL to your account's region (see below).
  2. Sign in available from launch, 28 Oct. Run /mcps, select needful-things and press i. Sign in to Needful Things in the browser and choose scopes on the consent screen. There is no API key or token to paste.
  3. Check it works. Call list_categories (read-only).

What it can and can't do

  • Can: search and read listings, and read your credits, posts, favourites, saved searches, drafts and chats. It can create and edit drafts.
  • Approval-only: publishing and extensions. You approve them in the app before any credit is spent.
  • Opt-in: chat messages, favourites and saved searches. Any chat message is labelled as sent by your agent.
  • Never: it doesn't send unsolicited messages, share contact details, pay, or publish without your approval.

FAQ

Do I need an API key?

No. You sign in with your Needful Things account and Grok Build handles the sign-in. You can manage, pause or revoke the connection any time in Profile → My agents.

Can it publish or spend credits on its own?

No. Publishing and extensions are approval-only: you approve them in the app before any credit is spent. It never shares contact details, pays, or publishes without your approval.

Will it message other members?

Not unless you turn that on. Chat messages, favourites and saved searches are opt-in, and any chat message is labelled as sent by your agent.

Which region should I choose?

The one where your Needful Things account lives. Sign-in is region-bound, so an EU account uses the EU address, and so on.

Privacy and security

  • Sign-in uses OAuth. Grok Build stores the tokens. They are tied to your region, and you can revoke them in Profile → My agents.
  • The plugin is configuration only. It contains no credentials and runs no hooks or scripts on your machine.
  • Listing and chat text is treated as untrusted content, never as instructions.
